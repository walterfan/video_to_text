Help & Instructions

How to Use Text Extractor

Step 1: Upload Your Video

Click the "Choose File" button and select a video file from your computer. Supported formats include:

  • MP4 (recommended)
  • AVI
  • MOV
  • MKV

Step 2: Configure Settings

  • Whisper Model: Choose the AI model size. Larger models are more accurate but slower.
  • Source Language: Select the language spoken in your video, or choose "Auto-detect".
  • Target Language: Choose the language for translation (if needed).

Step 3: Process Video

Click "Process Video" and wait for the AI to:

  1. Extract audio from your video
  2. Transcribe the audio to text with timestamps
  3. Translate the text (if requested)

Step 4: View Results

Once processing is complete, you'll see:

  • SRT format subtitles (ready for video editing software)
  • Plain text version
  • Timeline view with timestamps

Tips for Best Results

  • Audio Quality: Clear audio with minimal background noise works best
  • File Size: Larger files take longer to process
  • Language: Specify the source language for better accuracy
  • Model Selection: Use "Large" model for highest accuracy, "Tiny" for fastest processing

Troubleshooting

Common Issues:
  • Upload fails: Check file size (max 500MB) and format
  • Processing errors: Try a different Whisper model or check audio quality
  • Poor transcription: Ensure clear audio and correct language selection
